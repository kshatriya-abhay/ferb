# Format (as datetime.txt): now | tool | expected | request   (split on " | " at most 3 times)
# - now: "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM Ddd", local time; the date is authoritative, Ddd is a check.
# - tool: add_reminder, set_alarm, set_timer, add_event or show_agenda.
# - expected: a moment "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM"; a repeat "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM rule" (the first occurrence

# - request: as a person would type or say it: English, Hinglish in Latin letters, Hindi in Devanagari.
#
# Rules (the user's decisions):
# - A weekday, bare or with this/coming/next (agle), is the upcoming one: today only if its time is
#   still ahead, otherwise next week. "next <weekday>" said on that weekday is +7; said on another
#   day it is the coming one ("next Saturday" on a Wednesday is 3 days on).
# - A weekday, date, "tomorrow" or "in N days" with no time is 09:00. "Today" with no time asks.
# - Parts of the day with no hour: morning/subah 09:00, afternoon/dopahar 14:00, evening/shaam 18:00,
#   night/raat and tonight 20:00. With an hour: subah is am; dopahar, shaam and raat are pm (raat 12
#   is midnight).
# - Alarms always need a time; an alarm without one asks.
# - Alarms with a bare hour (no am/pm, part of the day or wake word) follow the user's alarm rules:
#   with a day given (tomorrow, kal, a weekday, a date), 5-11 are am, 12 is noon, 1-4 are pm;
#   with no day (or "today"), the candidate inside 06:00-18:00 if still ahead, else the other half
#   if still ahead today, else tomorrow by the day-given rule. Never a time already past today.
# - Wake words (wake, get up, utha, uthana, jaga) make the hour am.
# - Other bare hours (reminders, events): with a day said, 7-11 are am, 12 is noon,
#   1-6 are pm; with no day, it is the next time that hour comes round (am or pm, today or tomorrow).
# - A clock time or part of the day with no day that has passed today rolls to tomorrow; a time said for today
#   (aaj/today/tonight) that has passed asks.
# - kal is always tomorrow, even just after midnight; parso is +2, narso is +3.
# - "in N minutes/hours" counts exactly from now; sava +15, saade +30, paune -15, dedh 1.5.
# - noon is 12:00; midnight is 00:00 at the start of the next day.
# - Dates are day-first (en-IN): 5/1 is 5 January. A date with no year or month rolls forward to its
#   next occurrence ("the 5th" on the 20th is next month's; "25 Dec" on 26 Dec is next year's).
#   A date that is explicitly past (with a year) or doesn't exist (30 Feb, 31 April) asks.
# - An event's time is its start. Meetings, appointments (doctor, dentist, physio), classes, gym,
#   interviews, parties and meals with someone are events; "remind me" / "yaad dilana", including
#   daily medicine, are reminders.
# - Repeats: "every day", roz, har roz (daily); "every weekday", har weekday, Monday to Friday
#   (weekdays); every <weekday>, har <weekday> (weekly); every month on the Nth, har mahine N tareekh
#   (monthly). The expected value is the first occurrence after now.
