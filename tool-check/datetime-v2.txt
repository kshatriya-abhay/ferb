# Dates and times, v2 (2026-10-24): tool-check/datetime.txt (kept frozen) with one rule added by
# ferb-manager for the user, and lines for it at the end, written before the rule was built.
# Don't tune on it line by line; a change to a line or a rule below needs a new, dated file.
#
# Each line: now | tool | expected | request
#   now: the phone's clock, YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM and the weekday (checked by datetime.py)
#   expected: the resolved local datetime YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM; for show_agenda the day YYYY-MM-DD;
#     for set_timer the length as +PTnHnMnS; for a repeat the first time and the rule
#     (daily, weekdays, weekly); "ask" when Ferb should ask rather than guess.
#
# Rules (the user's, and ferb-manager's convention):
# - "next <weekday>", "agle <din>": the first such day after today; said on that weekday, a week on.
# - "this", "coming" or a bare weekday: the soonest such day whose time is still ahead; said on that
#   weekday after its time (09:00 when none is said), a week on.
# - today, tonight, tomorrow, the day after tomorrow; aaj, kal (tomorrow), parso (the day after) are
#   calendar days: just after midnight, "tomorrow" is the next calendar day.
# - in N minutes or hours: from now, exactly. In N days: that day, at 09:00 unless a time is said.
# - No time: reminders and events on a later day at 09:00; today with no time, ask; alarms always ask.
# - Parts of the day: morning 09:00, afternoon 14:00, evening 18:00, night and tonight 20:00. With an
#   hour: morning, subah = am; afternoon, dopahar, evening, shaam = pm; night, raat = pm (12-4 am).
#   "Tonight" when it's past 20:00, with no time: ask.
# - A bare hour (1-12, no am or pm or part of the day) with no day: the next time it comes, of h:00
#   and h+12:00. With a day said: 7-11 am, 12 noon, 1-6 pm.
# - (v2) Waking words (wake, get up, utha dena, jaga dena, uthana): a bare hour is am, with or
#   without a day; a part of the day said ("dopahar 2 baje utha dena") still decides.
# - noon 12:00; midnight 00:00 at the start of the next day; half past 6 is 6:30, quarter to 8 is 7:45.
# - A time said for today that has passed: ask. With no day, a passed time rolls to the next one.
# - Dates: "the 3rd" this month's, or next month's if passed; a date without a year the next one
#   (so across the year end); d/m is day/month (en-IN): 5/11 is 5 November.
# - Hindi times: saat baje 7, saade paanch 5:30.
